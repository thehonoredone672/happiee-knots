Our Story

Hi, I'm Abirakcha, the maker behind happiee.knots.

What started as a love for crochet has grown into a small business dedicated to creating handmade pieces with care and creativity. Every product is crocheted by me, one stitch at a time, making each piece unique and special.

At happiee.knots, my goal is simple—to create handmade gifts that bring joy, celebrate special moments, and make people smile.

Thank you for supporting my small business and choosing handmade. 💛🧶

All our hampers and products are carefully sourced, quality-checked, and packaged perfectly to ensure the receiver feels special. Whether it's a bulk corporate order or a single personalized mug, it's tied together with a happiee knot.
